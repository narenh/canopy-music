Multi-device Canopy Music: every screen

In the app, always the regular canopy logo; "canopy music" is the marketing logo.

Each zone has one hub (an iPad or an Apple TV) that plays the music and owns the queue and the base playlist. Usually there's one zone; a big house might have Upstairs and Downstairs, each with its own address. Admin happens in two places only: an iPad hub's host mode, and the web page with Host Control. A TV hub has no settings screens at all; it's managed entirely from the web page. Joined iPads and TVs are displays. On a display, search and add always work, reordering follows the hub's "Reorder from phones", and playback controls follow the hub's new "Playback controls on displays" (on by default). Nothing else: no delete, no playlist, no settings.

Creating a zone on an iPad: name it (defaults to the device name), then set a host PIN or skip. Asking up front should get more people to set one. On a TV: straight to "Scan to host"; the first host names the zone and picks the playlist from their phone.

Joined devices have no setup and no admin: joining goes straight to the party screen, and long-pressing the logo only offers Leave. Since joining grants nothing beyond what a guest's phone can do, it needs no PIN.

On launch, every device shows the zone list, even with one zone or none. Pick a zone to show it, or Create a new zone to make this device a hub. No auto-join.
