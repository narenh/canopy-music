          Connect Spotify

          Canopy Music plays Spotify through a developer app you create. It's free, and you only do this once.

          1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account. Playback needs Spotify Premium.
          2. If Spotify asks, accept the Developer Terms.
          3. Tap Create app.
          4. Give it any name and description, like “Canopy Music”.
          5. Under Redirect URIs, paste and tap Add.
          6. Under Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?, check Web API.
          7. Agree to the terms and tap Save.
          8. On your new app's page, open Settings and copy the Client ID.
          9. Paste it below.

          Client ID

          Your family can share one app if each person's email is added under User Management in the app's settings.
